# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I reset my password?
Go to Settings, then Security, then Reset Password. You'll receive
a reset link by email within two minutes. If it doesn't arrive,
check your spam folder.

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Enterprise
customers can also pay by bank transfer or invoice.

## Do you offer refunds?
Yes. We offer a full refund within 30 days of purchase, no
questions asked. Contact support@example.com to request one.

## How do I cancel my subscription?
Log in, go to Billing, and click "Cancel Subscription". Your
access continues until the end of the current billing period.
